CCFlex

Customize Control Center on iOS 26 - Tint glass backgrounds and adjust their opacity. - Recolor active fills and set their opacity, or hide supported switch fills. - Pick individual colors for supported switch backgrounds and icons. - See brightness and volume percentages in compact and expanded sliders. . Configure in Settings > CCFlex.

Compatibility

Requires jailbroken iOS 26. Choose the Rootless or native RootHide build for your jailbreak. iOS 27 support may be possible once a compatible jailbreak is available and CCFlex can be tested.

Install the native RootHide package directly without converting the rootless package.

Changelog

1.0.1 2026-11-09

  • Added Increase Contrast for Control Center. With the switch off, Control Center follows the system setting.
  • Settings previews now update when Increase Contrast is toggled, including solid fills.
  • Fixed custom icon colors for Amber Switch and other supported switches with image-based icons. Their previews now follow the same fill and background rules as built-in switches.
