WiFi RSSI Enabler

Show Wi-Fi RSSI (Received Signal Strength Indicator) in the status bar in place of the Wi-Fi bars. The number is in dBm: -50 is stronger than -70. iOS 16 and later.

iOS 16 and laterRootless and native RootHide1.0.3

Compatibility

Requires iOS 16 or later. Tested on iPhone 8 Plus (non-notched) with iOS 16.6 and Dopamine RootHide and on iPhone 11 Pro Max (notched) with iOS 26.0.1 and Dopamine 3 RootHide.

Install the native RootHide package directly without converting the rootless package.

Changelog
