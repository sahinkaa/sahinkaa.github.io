NeverLowerMusic16

Prevents notification sounds from lowering now-playing audio on iOS 16. During playback, notification volume follows media volume.

iOS 16Rootless only1.0.0

Compatibility

iOS 16.x only. Rootless package; tested on iPhone 8 Plus, iOS 16.6, Dopamine RootHide after conversion. No native RootHide release.

Only the published rootless build is hosted. This is not a native RootHide package.
