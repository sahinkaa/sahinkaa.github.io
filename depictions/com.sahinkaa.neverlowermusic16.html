Never Lower Music

Prevents notification sounds from lowering now-playing audio. During playback, notification volume follows media volume. Tested on iPhone 8 Plus (iOS 16.6, Dopamine RootHide via conversion) and iPhone 11 Pro Max (iOS 26.0.1, Dopamine 3 RootHide).

iOS 16.0 and laterRootless and native RootHide1.0.1

Compatibility

Installable on iOS 16.0 and later. Tested on iPhone 8 Plus with iOS 16.6 and Dopamine RootHide using the earlier converted package. The native build was tested on iPhone 11 Pro Max with iOS 26.0.1 and Dopamine 3 RootHide.

Install the native RootHide package directly without converting the rootless package.
