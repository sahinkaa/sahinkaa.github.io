ApnHistory Remade

ApnHistory Remade keeps encrypted, on-device history of app notifications, incoming SMS and calls. Browse by app or in a timeline, search as you type and copy notification text.

Choose what to log, protect important entries and set automatic cleanup rules. Use password-protected backups in Files to transfer settings, history or both.

Original ApnHistory by m156nrkvv. Remade by sahinkaa, using Vē Enhanced’s notification capture method.

Changelog

1.1.2 2026-11-26

  • Added a one-time cleanup for obsolete files left by the original ApnHistory. Your notification history and current settings are preserved.
  • Removed legacy code that recreated the old marker file and sent unused status bar notifications.

1.1.1 2026-11-22

  • Lowered the Per App and Timeline tabs and Settings gear to prevent status bar overlap on Dynamic Island devices.
  • Centered the tab divider on the screen and the Settings gear in the space to its right.
  • Added a small gap above the separator and expanded the header tap areas.

1.1.0 2026-11-21

  • Encrypted history storage. Existing notifications migrate to an encrypted SQLite database. New notifications are queued securely and imported when you open or refresh ApnHistory.
  • Logging and cleanup rules. Choose which apps and keywords to log or ignore. Set a maximum age or item count, create automatic cleanup rules and manage Auto Protect in one place. Set a rule’s range to 0 to disable it while keeping it in the list.
  • Filter and manage saved items. Filter by app, text and date with a live match count. Protect, unprotect or delete selected entries in bulk. Delete Selected skips protected entries.
  • Password-protected backups in Files. Back up and restore settings, history or both using On My iPhone or iCloud Drive. Restore merges history with existing entries and skips matching notifications.
  • Timeline navigation. Drag the side scrollbar to move through your history with a month and year readout. Light haptics mark month changes and the top and bottom of the list.
  • Per App groups. Groups start collapsed and reset when you return from Timeline. Tap an app to expand its history.
  • Layout and reading. Top controls respect the safe area. Tap a long notification to read the rest or hold it for actions.
  • Navigation, live search, backup and restore performed well with 80,000 history items on iPhone 8 Plus (iOS 16.6, Dopamine RootHide) and iPhone 11 Pro Max (iOS 26.0.1, Dopamine RootHide). A decade of history can fit in under 100 MB, depending on notification volume and content.

1.0.1 2026-11-15

  • Added live search to Per App and Timeline.
  • Tap an app header to expand or collapse its history. Counts show all saved items or only matches while searching.
  • Added a 0–100% blur strength control for photo backgrounds.
  • Set Maximum Items Saved to 0 for unlimited history or enter a custom limit, including values above 10,000.
  • Renamed Settings controls to Log App Notifications, Log SMS, Log Calls, Solid Color and Blur Effect.
